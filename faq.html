Frequently Asked Questions

What are your session fees?

My session fee is $200 per 50 minute session for individual sessions, and $250 per 50 minute session for couples and family support sessions. No tax will be applied on top of these fees.

Do you offer a sliding scale?

While I do offer a sliding scale, I currently have a waitlist for sliding scale spots. Feel free to inquire regarding a waitlist position for a sliding scale spot.

Are you accepting new clients?

Yes, I am currently accepting new clients. I offer both virtual and in-person sessions.

Do you require a credit card on file?

Yes, a credit card is required to book appointments and allows payment to be processed securely after each session.

How can I schedule a session?

All scheduling is done through email — send a message via the contact form or email info@taylermiddleton.com directly to set up a free 15 minute phone consultation.
